DIGI-GRANTHANTYESHTI

Giving digital documents a proper farewell

We create, collect, and accumulate digital documents throughout our lives. Notes, manuscripts, photographs, videos, and other files become part of our digital world. Sometimes, it is time to let them go.

Digi-GranthAntyeshti brings a touch of intention and ceremony to that final act.

What does the name mean?

Antyeshti is a traditional final rite — a formal way of bringing something to its end.

A grantha is a written work, text, book, or manuscript. In the digital world, the idea extends beyond text documents to include images, videos, and other digital content.

So why shouldn't your digital grantha receive a proper final disposition too?
